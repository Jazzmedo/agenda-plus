# Changelog

## Unreleased

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-21

- Month, week and day calendar views: events as coloured bars, tasks as chips, drag a task
  to another day to reschedule it.
- A Today view with overdue rollover, a completion ring, quick-add and an undated backlog.
- A Stats view: completed/open/overdue KPIs, completion rate, streak, per-day trend, a
  12-week consistency heatmap, and breakdowns by tag and priority.
- Tasks are ordinary markdown checkboxes with `obsidian-tasks`-compatible emoji metadata —
  nothing is locked in a database.
- Recurring tasks with a structured repeat picker: intervals, weekly day toggles, monthly
  anchor days, "ends on date / at event", `when done`, and a live preview of coming dates.
- Event linking: a repeating task can count down to an event (`🎯`) and stop on its date.
- Priority by tag with colours, calendar filters, and per-tag settings.
- Events as notes with frontmatter, including multi-day spans.
